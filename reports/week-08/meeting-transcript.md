# Week 8 meeting transcript

**The Sprint Review has not been held yet, so there is no transcript.**

This file exists so the coverage table in [the week's report](README.md#coverage) has the row the assignment asks for, and so that whoever holds the meeting knows where the transcript goes.

After the meeting, this file becomes one of three things, decided by the answers to the permission questions in [the script](meeting-script.md#part-1-permission-questions-2-minutes):

- **The transcript**, when the `Customer` agrees to both recording and publication.
- **One line saying the `Customer` refused publication**, in which case the transcript goes to the Moodle submission wrapper instead and never to this repository.
- **One line saying why there is none**, when the meeting was not recorded or was held in writing.

No transcript is published without his answer to question 2, which is separate from his answer to question 1: he can agree to being recorded and refuse publication, and the script asks them separately for that reason.
