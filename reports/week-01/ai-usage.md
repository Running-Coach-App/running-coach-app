# AI usage — Week 01

## Tools

Claude (Claude Code, Opus model, Claude Chat, Gemini AI), used by all members through the VS Code extension and web browser.

## What we used them for

- Reading the course requirements and turning the Week 1 checklist into the repository structure: `LICENSE`, `.gitignore`, the pull request template, the Lychee workflow, and `dependabot.yml`.
- A first pass of desk research: collecting candidate products and the URLs of their help-center, pricing, and documentation pages for [the candidate list](candidate-list.md) and [the alternatives](../../docs/research/alternatives.md).
- Drafting the structure and first wording of the comparison table, the gap analysis, the value propositions, and the meeting script.

## What we did with the output

- Every URL in the research files was opened in a browser by a team member, and every claim was checked against the page it cites.
  Claims we could not confirm were deleted or marked as "could not verify".
- The property set was fixed by the team before the comparison was filled in.
- The hands-on parts of the evaluation (installing the apps, going through onboarding, taking screenshots for the board) were done by team members, not by the tool.
- The meeting script questions were re-read aloud by the interviewer and rewritten where they did not sound like something we would say.
- The meeting report and the transcript were written by the team from the meeting itself.

## What was not used

No AI output was used as a finding without a source a team member opened.
No generated text was submitted unchecked.
