# FR-011: Keep The Finished Runs And Show Them On Request

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-10`](https://github.com/Running-Coach-App/running-coach-app/issues/141), [`ADR-001`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-001.md)
- **Containers:** `coach app`

## Behavior

When a session stops, the product shall record that session with the date it ran, the distance it covered, and its elapsed time.

The product shall keep those records across a restart of the app and of the phone, and shall show them on request, most recent first.

When no session has been recorded, the product shall say so rather than showing an empty list.

The product shall keep the records on the phone and shall send them nowhere.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a session that has been stopped, when the runner opens the history, then that session appears with its date, its distance, and its elapsed time.

### AC-02

Given a session recorded earlier, when the app is closed and opened again, then that session still appears in the history.

### AC-03

Given a phone on which no session has been recorded, when the runner opens the history, then the product says there are no runs yet and shows no rows.
