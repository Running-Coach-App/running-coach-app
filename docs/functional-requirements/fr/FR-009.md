# FR-009: Speak Heart Rate When A Wearable Is Already Connected

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-07`](https://github.com/Running-Coach-App/running-coach-app/issues/43), [`DEC-002`](../../decisions.md#dec-002), [`CON-04`](../../product-vision.md#con-04), [`ADR-001`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-001.md), [`ADR-002`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-002.md)
- **Containers:** `coach app`

## Behavior

When a wearable that reports heart rate is already connected to the phone, the product shall speak the heart rate alongside the time, the pace, and the distance in each stats line.

When no such wearable is connected, the product shall speak the stats line without a heart rate, and shall not say that the heart rate is missing.

The product shall not require a wearable, shall not ask the runner to connect one, and shall not pair with one itself.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a wearable reporting heart rate is connected before the session starts, when a stats line falls due, then the line carries a heart rate as well as the time, the pace, and the distance.

### AC-02

Given no wearable is connected, when a stats line falls due, then the line carries the time, the pace, and the distance, and says nothing about a heart rate.

### AC-03

Given a wearable that disconnects during a session, when the next stats line falls due, then the line carries the remaining stats and the session continues.
