# FR-004: Answer A Spoken Question About The Current Run

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-04`](https://github.com/Running-Coach-App/running-coach-app/issues/40), [`DEC-008`](../../decisions.md#dec-008), [`DEC-016`](../../decisions.md#dec-016), [`DEC-040`](../../decisions.md#dec-040), [`ADR-001`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-001.md), [`ADR-004`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-004.md)
- **Containers:** `coach app`

## Behavior

While a session is running, when the runner asks aloud about the elapsed time, the pace, or the distance of that session, the product shall speak the stats the question asked for.

The product shall accept more than one wording of the same question, and shall not require the runner to learn a fixed list of commands.

When a question asks for more than one stat, the product shall speak each stat the question asked for, and when a question asks for the stats as a whole, the product shall speak all three.

The wordings the product accepts are data rather than code, in `app/src/main/java/app/runningcoach/QuestionVocabulary.kt`, so a wording a runner used and the product missed is added as a line there. [`DEC-034`](../../decisions.md#dec-034) is why they are generated rather than guessed at.

When no session is running, the product shall say that there is no session rather than answering with a stat.

The product shall not capture a spoken question while it is speaking a line. A question asked during that line is not heard; the runner asks after the line finishes. [`DEC-040`](../../decisions.md#dec-040) is why.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a session running for twelve minutes, when the runner asks how long they have been running, then the coach speaks the elapsed time of that session.

### AC-02

Given a session with a location fix, when the runner asks how far they have gone, then the coach speaks the distance.

### AC-03

Given a session, when the runner asks for the time and the pace in one question, then the coach speaks both.

### AC-04

Given no session is running, when the runner asks how long they have been running, then the coach says there is no session and speaks no stat.

### AC-05

Given a session, when the runner asks for the stats as a whole rather than for one of them, then the coach speaks the time, the pace, and the distance in one line.

### AC-06

Given a session, when the runner names one stat in a question that also contains an everyday word, then the coach speaks that stat and not the others.

### AC-07

Given the coach is speaking a line, when the runner asks a question during that line, then the product does not hear it. The runner asks after the line finishes.
