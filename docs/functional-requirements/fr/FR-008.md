# FR-008: Offer Two Preset Sessions

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-06`](https://github.com/Running-Coach-App/running-coach-app/issues/42), [`VP-02`](../../research/value-proposition.md#vp-02), [`ADR-001`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-001.md), [`ADR-002`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-002.md)
- **Containers:** `coach app`

## Behavior

The product shall offer the runner exactly two ready-made sessions to choose from: one of steady effort, and one of changing effort.

When the runner chooses the steady session, the product shall speak that session's stats lines through to the stop and shall speak no effort cue.

When the runner chooses the changing session, the product shall speak an effort cue at each change of effort, per [`FR-003`](FR-003.md).

The product shall not ask the runner to describe or configure a session of their own.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given no session is running, when the runner looks for a session to start, then exactly two are offered.

### AC-02

Given the steady session, when the runner starts it and runs for nine minutes, then the coach has spoken its stats lines and no effort cue.

### AC-03

Given the changing session, when the runner starts it and runs for three minutes, then the coach has spoken a harder effort and an easier effort.
