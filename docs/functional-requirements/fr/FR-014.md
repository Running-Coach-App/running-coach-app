# FR-014: Report What The Coach Did During A Session

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-13`](https://github.com/Running-Coach-App/running-coach-app/issues/168), [`ADR-002`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-002.md)
- **Containers:** `coach app`

## Behavior

The product shall write one structured record for every line that falls due during a session, whether the line was spoken or not.

Each record shall carry the moment the line fell due, an identifier for the line, the kind of line, the outcome, the delay in milliseconds between the line falling due and the speech starting or being abandoned, and the session the line belongs to.

The outcome shall distinguish a line that was spoken from one that was dropped because the previous line was still playing and one that was dropped because the speech engine was unavailable, so a failure can be told from a success and one failure from another.

The product shall write the records on the device and shall send them nowhere. It shall keep the last seven days or the last fifty sessions, whichever is reached first, and drop older records.

No record shall carry a name, an address, free text, or anything the runner said.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a session in which the coach speaks a stats line, when the line has been spoken, then a record exists for it carrying its identifier, its kind, an outcome of spoken, and a delay in milliseconds.

### AC-02

Given a line that falls due while the previous line is still playing, when the line is abandoned, then a record exists for it with an outcome that says it was dropped because the speech was busy, and with the delay up to the moment it was abandoned.

### AC-03

Given a session that has finished, when its records are read back, then every line that fell due during it appears exactly once, in the order the lines fell due.

### AC-04

Given any record the product has written, when its fields are read, then none of them carries a name, an address, free text, or anything the runner said.
