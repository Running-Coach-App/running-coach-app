# FR-007: Speak The Run's Summary Once It Has Stopped

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-05`](https://github.com/Running-Coach-App/running-coach-app/issues/41), [`ADR-001`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-001.md), [`ADR-002`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-002.md)
- **Containers:** `coach app`

## Behavior

When the runner stops a session, the product shall speak the distance covered and the elapsed time of that session once.

The product shall speak the summary after the line confirming that the session has stopped, and shall speak no further cue for that session afterwards.

When the session covered no distance, the product shall say that it has no distance rather than speaking a number.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a session that covered some distance, when the runner stops it, then the coach speaks the distance and the elapsed time once.

### AC-02

Given a session with no location fix throughout, when the runner stops it, then the coach says it has no distance and speaks no distance figure.

### AC-03

Given a session that has been stopped and summarised, when a further three minutes pass, then the coach speaks nothing.
