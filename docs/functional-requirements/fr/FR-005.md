# FR-005: Reply To An Off-Topic Question Without Inventing An Answer

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-04`](https://github.com/Running-Coach-App/running-coach-app/issues/40), [`DEC-012`](../../decisions.md#dec-012), [`ADR-004`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-004.md)
- **Containers:** `coach app`

## Behavior

While a session is running, when the runner asks something that is not about the elapsed time, the pace, or the distance of that session, the product shall reply with a short spoken line that turns the runner back to the run.

The product shall not answer such a question with a fact of its own, and shall not stay silent.

The product shall keep the session running and shall speak its scheduled lines as usual after such a reply.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a session is running, when the runner asks what the weather is, then the coach speaks a line that turns the runner back to the run and states no weather.

### AC-02

Given a session is running, when the runner asks an off-topic question, then the coach does not stop the session and speaks the next scheduled stats line at its due time.

### AC-03

Given a session is running, when the runner asks an off-topic question, then the reply is one short spoken line rather than silence.
