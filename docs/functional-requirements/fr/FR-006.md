# FR-006: Announce A Missing Stat Once And Then Omit It

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-02`](https://github.com/Running-Coach-App/running-coach-app/issues/38), [`DEC-011`](../../decisions.md#dec-011), [`ADR-001`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-001.md)
- **Containers:** `coach app`

## Behavior

When a stat the product would speak is not available, the product shall say once that it is not available.

While that stat stays unavailable, the product shall leave it out of the following lines rather than repeating that it is not available.

When the stat becomes available again, the product shall speak it in the next line, and shall say once more that it is unavailable if it is lost again.

The product shall keep speaking the stats that are available throughout.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a session with no location fix, when the first stats line is due, then the coach says once that the pace and the distance are not available.

### AC-02

Given a session whose pace and distance have already been announced as unavailable, when the next two stats lines are due, then each line carries the elapsed time and neither repeats that the pace and the distance are unavailable.

### AC-03

Given a session whose location fix returns after being lost, when the next stats line is due, then that line carries the pace and the distance.
