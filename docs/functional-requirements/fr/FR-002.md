# FR-002: Speak The Run's Stats On A Fixed Cadence

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-02`](https://github.com/Running-Coach-App/running-coach-app/issues/38), [`DEC-013`](../../decisions.md#dec-013), [`ADR-001`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-001.md), [`ADR-002`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-002.md)
- **Containers:** `coach app`

## Behavior

While a session is running, the product shall speak the elapsed time, the pace, and the distance of that session on a fixed cadence of three minutes.

The product shall speak each stats line without the runner asking for it.

The product shall keep the cadence fixed for the duration of the session, and shall not offer the runner a setting that changes it.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a steady session that has been running for nine minutes with a location fix, when the session is replayed, then the coach has spoken three stats lines, one every three minutes.

### AC-02

Given a stats line is due, when the coach speaks it, then the line carries the elapsed time, the pace, and the distance, in that order.

### AC-03

Given no session is running, when three minutes pass, then the coach speaks no stats line.
