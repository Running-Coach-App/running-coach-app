# FR-002: Speak The Run's Stats Without Being Asked

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-02`](https://github.com/Running-Coach-App/running-coach-app/issues/38), [`DEC-013`](../../decisions.md#dec-013), [`DEC-036`](../../decisions.md#dec-036), [`ADR-001`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-001.md), [`ADR-002`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-002.md)
- **Containers:** `coach app`

## Behavior

While a session is running, the product shall speak the elapsed time, the pace, and the distance of that session at the interval [`FR-012`](FR-012.md) gives, which shall be three minutes until the runner sets one.

The product shall speak each stats line without the runner asking for it.

The product shall keep the interval fixed for the duration of a session, and shall apply a changed interval from the start of the next one.

Which intervals the runner may choose, and how the choice is kept, are [`FR-012`](FR-012.md).

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a steady session that has been running for nine minutes with a location fix and a cadence the runner has not changed, when the session is replayed, then the coach has spoken three stats lines, one every three minutes.

### AC-02

Given a stats line is due, when the coach speaks it, then the line carries the elapsed time, the pace, and the distance, in that order.

### AC-03

Given no session is running, when three minutes pass, then the coach speaks no stats line.

### AC-04

Given a session that is already running, when the runner changes the interval, then the running session keeps the interval it started with, and the next session uses the new one.
