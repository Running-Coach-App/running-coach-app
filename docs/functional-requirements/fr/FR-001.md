# FR-001: Start And Stop A Session Without The Screen

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-01`](https://github.com/Running-Coach-App/running-coach-app/issues/37), [`DEC-015`](../../decisions.md#dec-015), [`CON-04`](../../product-vision.md#con-04), [`ADR-001`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-001.md)
- **Containers:** `coach app`

## Behavior

When the runner gives the start control for an interval session or for a steady session, the product shall begin that session and shall say that the session has started.

When the runner gives the stop control during a session, the product shall end the session, shall say that the session has stopped, and shall speak no further cue for that session.

The product shall not require the runner to unlock or look at the screen to start or stop a session.

The product shall not start or stop a session from detected motion alone.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given no session is running, when the runner gives the start control for a steady session, then the coach says that the session has started and the session clock begins.

### AC-02

Given a session is running, when the runner gives the stop control, then the coach says that the session has stopped and speaks no scheduled line afterwards.

### AC-03

Given a session is running with the screen off, when the runner gives the stop control without waking the screen, then the session ends.
