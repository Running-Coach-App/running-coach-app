# ADR-002: Speak With The Platform Text-To-Speech Voice

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Created:** 2026-10-22
- **Updated:** 2026-10-22
- **Containers:** `coach app`
- **Addresses:** [`FR-002`](../../functional-requirements/fr/FR-002.md), [`FR-003`](../../functional-requirements/fr/FR-003.md), [`QR-01`](../../quality-requirements/qr/QR-01.md), [`QR-04`](../../quality-requirements/qr/QR-04.md)
- **Decision:** [`DEC-009`](../../decisions.md#dec-009)

## Context

Every line the coach says has to reach a runner who is not looking at the screen, and the lines are short and plain: an elapsed time, a pace, a distance, and one of three effort words. [`QR-01`](../../quality-requirements/qr/QR-01.md) puts a two-second bound between the moment a line falls due and the moment it starts playing, and [`CON-03`](../../product-vision.md#con-03) keeps the run on the phone.

A voice can come from three places, and the choice changes what ships in the repository, what the application has to load before a session starts, and whether a runner out of signal hears anything at all.

## Considered Options

- **A cloud text-to-speech service.** The best-sounding voices, and no weights in the repository. It loses because every line would cross the network, which breaks the two-second bound whenever signal is poor and contradicts [`CON-03`](../../product-vision.md#con-03); a runner out of signal would hear nothing.
- **A neural voice bundled in the application.** A voice the team controls exactly, offline. It loses on size and on time: the weights would have to live in the repository or be fetched on first run, and the team has no week to spend tuning a voice for lines this plain.
- **The Android platform text-to-speech engine.**

## Decision Outcome

The Android platform text-to-speech engine. The application asks the platform for a voice at startup and speaks through it.

It beats the other two because the lines are short and factual, where a platform voice is already good enough, and because the engine is on the device: no network, nothing added to the repository, and nothing to download before a first run. It is the only option that holds the two-second bound in the environment the runner is actually in.

## Consequences

The coach's voice is whatever voice the runner has installed, so it differs between phones and the team cannot promise one sound.

The application depends on a platform capability it does not own. A phone with no English voice installed cannot run the coach, which `docs/setup.md` states as a prerequisite rather than something the product fixes.

The decision accepts these quality attributes:

- **Time behaviour**, covered by [`QR-01`](../../quality-requirements/qr/QR-01.md): speech is produced on the device, so the bound is met without a network in the path.
- **Fault tolerance**, covered by [`QR-04`](../../quality-requirements/qr/QR-04.md): the voice keeps working while the location fix is gone, so a session with missing stats can still speak the ones it has.
