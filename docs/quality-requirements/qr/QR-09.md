# QR-09: Start A Coached Session Without Being Shown How

A runner who has never seen the product works out how to start a coached session, hear it through, and end it, without being told and without taking the phone back out of a pocket.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Interaction Capability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Learnability
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium

`Learnability` is the sub-characteristic because the thing in doubt is what the product teaches a runner in its first minute. The product has one screen of instruction — a single line of text saying which hardware control starts which session — and then it expects the phone to go into a pocket and stay there. Nothing explains itself after that: there is no tutorial, no second prompt, and by design nothing to look at. Either the first minute was enough or the run is spent confused, and no amount of operability makes up for it.

The measure that reads this is **whether a participant completes the main task `Unaided`**. It is the right one for learnability because `Unaided` is defined as reaching the end state with no help from anybody, which is exactly the condition of a runner alone with the product for the first time. The moment a facilitator gives a hint, the session stops measuring what the product taught and starts measuring what the team taught. The other three measures the sessions record say other things — the SEQ says how hard it felt, the time says how long it took, the SUS says what the whole impression was — and all three are reported as results, but none of them distinguishes a participant who worked it out from one who was told.

[`QR-03`](QR-03.md) is the neighbour, and the pair is deliberate rather than a duplication. `QR-03` is `Operability`: it asks whether every step of a session can be *reached* without the screen, and it is measured by a runner taking the phone out zero times. This requirement asks whether a stranger *discovers* those steps unaided. A product can pass one and fail the other: controls that are reachable but undiscoverable pass `QR-03` and fail this, and a well-signposted flow that needs the screen fails `QR-03` and passes this.

## Scenario

### Source

A representative participant in [`UT-01`](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md): an adult who runs or walks briskly for exercise at least occasionally, takes a phone with them when they do, has used some phone app while exercising, is not on the team, and has never seen this product.

### Stimulus

The participant is given the phone unlocked with the app open, is read the main task's scenario once, and is asked to be coached through a short run with the phone in a pocket and to finish the session when they have had enough. They are told nothing about the product and nothing about the interface, and the facilitator answers no question about it.

### Environment

A `UT-01` session, in person, outdoors on or beside the university campus, in daylight and dry weather. The team's Android phone with headphones, running the usability build, with the app's data cleared beforehand so the product has never been run. No network is needed, and the product holds no permission to use one.

### Artifact

The product's first screen and its session controls, and the spoken lines the coach produces during a session.

### Response

The participant shall reach the main task's end state — a session started, the coach heard at least once while running, and the session ended, with the phone staying in the pocket from the moment they put it there — without help, within the task's 12-minute limit.

### Response Measure

Across the counted `UT-01` sessions, **the proportion of participants who complete the main task `Unaided` is at least 0.6.**

The statistic is the proportion of counted participants whose main-task outcome is `Unaided`, which [the analysis plan](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#analysis-plan) reports as the main task's aggregate completion. `Unaided`, `Assisted`, and `Failed` are defined for the main task in [the record](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#main-task-be-coached-through-a-short-run-with-the-phone-away), and the help that separates the first from the second is defined in [the help rule](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#the-help-rule), both written before the first session.

At the minimum of three counted sessions the bar is 2 of 3, which is 0.67. At the design target of five it is 3 of 5, which is 0.6. One participant in three, or two in five, may need a hint and the product still clears it.

**Why that bar and not another.** It is set where the answer changes, not where the team would like it to be. The product's promise is a coach a runner does not look at; if most first-time runners cannot get there on their own, the promise is not kept, and 0.6 is the lowest proportion at which "most" is still true. Setting it at 1.0 would fail the product for one confused participant in five, which a sample this size cannot distinguish from chance. Setting it at 0.5 would pass a product that half of its users cannot start.

The bar is not a target. Participants are not coached toward it: the facilitator's script and the help rule exist to stop exactly that, and the hint is said aloud onto the recording so that an `Assisted` outcome cannot quietly become an `Unaided` one. The bar was written before the first session and is not changed after the data; if the result is to be argued with, it is argued with in the findings.

## QRT-01

The main task of [`UT-01`](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md) is run with at least three representative participants, and the proportion that completed it unaided is read from the results.

- **Status:** Active
- **Level:** `manual`
- **Runs at:** [`UT-01`](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md), in the counted sessions the record schedules, read from [`results.md`](../../usability-tests/ut-01/results.md).

**Why Manual:** no command can judge this measure. The measure is whether a participant reached the end state *without help*, and help is a thing one person gives another: a pointed finger, a read-aloud label, a confirmed guess. Only the observer in the session sees whether it happened. The product's own records cannot supply it either — the phone records that a session started and that the coach spoke, not whether somebody beside the runner had just told them how, and [the record says so](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#what-the-instrumentation-cannot-cover). Nor is the end state itself machine-readable: it includes the phone staying in the pocket, which no event on the phone can distinguish from the phone being in a hand.

This test reads no `SLI`. [The observability page](../../observability.md) has none for it, and there could not be one: a service level indicator is computed from what the product reports, and what is measured here is what a person did and was not told.

The test needs no deviation. The level is `manual` because the measure is a human judgement made in a scheduled session, which is what `manual` is for.

### Procedure

1. Confirm the design is merged and unchanged since: [the record](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md), the tasks, the help rule, and this bar were all written before the first session.
2. Confirm the instrumentation is implemented and verified, per [the record](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#the-verification-before-the-first-counted-session). The verification does not supply the measure; it is what lets the results be checked against the phone's own record.
3. Run the main task with at least three counted participants — five is the design target — each matching [the participant profile](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#participant-profile), each with the durable consent collected, each on a phone whose data was cleared, and all facilitated by the same member, per [the protocol](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#protocol).
4. For each session, the observer records the main task's outcome as `Unaided`, `Assisted`, or `Failed` against the criterion in the record, and writes down every hint given with the moment it was given.
5. Write the per-participant table in [`results.md`](../../usability-tests/ut-01/results.md), and compute the proportion of counted participants whose main-task outcome is `Unaided`: that count divided by the number of counted sessions.
6. Compare that proportion against the bar of 0.6, and record the verdict in [the quality requirement testing plan](../../project-plan.md#quality-requirement-testing-plan) with `Checked: SPRINT-08` and `results.md` as its evidence. The verdict is recorded in the plan and not in `results.md`.
7. Exclude from the count any session that did not count: a dry run, a withdrawn session, one without the required consent or recording, or one stopped before its data was complete. Record each retired code and its reason in the results.

**Pass when:** at least 0.6 of the counted `UT-01` participants completed the main task `Unaided` — 2 of 3 at the minimum of three counted sessions, 3 of 5 at the design target of five — with the outcomes judged against the criterion and the help rule written in the record before the first session. **Fails** when that proportion is below 0.6. **Not verified** until at least three sessions have been counted.
