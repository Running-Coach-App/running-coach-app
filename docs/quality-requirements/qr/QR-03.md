# QR-03: Run A Whole Session Without Looking At The Screen

A runner starts a session, hears it through, and stops it without taking the phone out or looking at the screen.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Interaction Capability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Operability
- **Business importance:** Medium
- **Technical risk:** High

## Scenario

### Source

A beginner to intermediate runner with the phone in a jacket pocket and headphones on.

### Stimulus

The runner starts a session, runs for at least ten minutes, asks one question aloud, and stops the session.

### Environment

Outdoors, phone in a pocket with the screen off, headphones connected, and no wearable, which [`CON-04`](../../product-vision.md#con-04) makes the baseline.

### Artifact

The session controls and the spoken replies of the running app.

### Response

Every step of the session shall be reachable without the runner seeing the screen, through a control the runner can feel or speak to, and every reply shall be spoken.

### Response Measure

In a timed walkthrough, a runner who is not on the team completes start, one spoken question, and stop with the phone in the pocket throughout, taking it out zero times.
