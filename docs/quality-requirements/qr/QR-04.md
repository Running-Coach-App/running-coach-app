# QR-04: Keep Coaching When The Location Fix Is Lost

A session keeps speaking the stats it still has when the phone loses its location fix, instead of stopping or going silent.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Reliability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Fault Tolerance
- **Business importance:** Medium
- **Technical risk:** Low

## Scenario

### Source

The phone's location provider, which stops returning a fix.

### Stimulus

The runner passes under a bridge or between tall buildings and the fix is lost for several minutes, then returns.

### Environment

A session running with the screen off, with the elapsed time still available from the clock while the pace and the distance are not.

### Artifact

The stats the running session speaks.

### Response

The session shall continue, shall keep speaking the stats that are still available, and shall resume the missing stats once the fix returns, without the runner restarting the session.

### Response Measure

Across a loss of fix of at least three minutes, the session speaks every scheduled line, each one carrying the elapsed time, and no line is skipped; the pace and the distance reappear within one scheduled line of the fix returning.
