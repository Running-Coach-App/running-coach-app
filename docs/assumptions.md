# Assumptions

## ASM-01

The first audience is beginner to intermediate, and marathon runners are out of the first release.

- **Status:** Confirmed
- **How to check:** ask the `Customer` who the first release is for.
- **Outcome:** he named beginner to intermediate and said marathon runners may have requirements he does not know, per [`DEC-001`](decisions.md#dec-001), in [the kickoff meeting](../reports/week-01/meeting-report.md#decisions).

## ASM-02

The product works from the phone alone, and a wearable only adds signals such as heart rate.

- **Status:** Confirmed
- **How to check:** ask the `Customer` whether a watch is required.
- **Outcome:** phone-only is the baseline and a wearable is worth exploring rather than required, per [`DEC-002`](decisions.md#dec-002), in [the kickoff meeting](../reports/week-01/meeting-report.md#decisions).

## ASM-03

Runners will not upload medical documents or type extra health data in.

- **Status:** Confirmed
- **How to check:** ask the `Customer` whether users will upload health or medical documents.
- **Outcome:** they will not — users are either too lazy or treat it as a security problem — so there is no perceived-effort step in the first release, per [`DEC-003`](decisions.md#dec-003), in [the kickoff meeting](../reports/week-01/meeting-report.md#decisions).

## ASM-04

Processing stays on the phone where possible.

- **Status:** Confirmed
- **How to check:** ask the `Customer` whether the run may be sent to a server.
- **Outcome:** he would rather it were processed on the phone, per [`DEC-004`](decisions.md#dec-004), in [the kickoff meeting](../reports/week-01/meeting-report.md#decisions).

## ASM-05

Sharing stats with a friend is out of scope for these ten weeks.

- **Status:** Confirmed
- **How to check:** propose live sharing with a running partner and see whether it is accepted.
- **Outcome:** refused on effort grounds and left open as a later paid tier, per [`DEC-005`](decisions.md#dec-005), in [the kickoff meeting](../reports/week-01/meeting-report.md#decisions).

## ASM-06

The first release is Android and English.

- **Status:** Confirmed
- **How to check:** ask the `Customer` which platform and language the first version should use.
- **Outcome:** Android, not iOS, because he has no iOS device to test, and English, per [`DEC-006`](decisions.md#dec-006), in [the kickoff meeting](../reports/week-01/meeting-report.md#decisions).

## ASM-07

The base product is the in-run voice coach, and a preset session comes only after it.

- **Status:** Open
- **How to check:** ask the `Customer` at the Week 2 validation meeting whether an adaptive training plan is wanted at all, per action point A2 in [the kickoff meeting](../reports/week-01/meeting-report.md#action-points).

## ASM-08

Fixed spoken cues are acceptable for the first release, and a conversational coach is not required to ship.

- **Status:** Open
- **How to check:** ask the `Customer` directly at the Week 2 validation meeting, per action point A4 in [the kickoff meeting](../reports/week-01/meeting-report.md#action-points).
